**InFaeStation** was developed in Unity C# for the Global Game Jam 2023 (held under the theme "Roots"). Players take on the role of a tiny "Rootling" brought to life to save its tree from an incoming bug infestation.

## Core Technical & Design Elements

1. **Top-Down Action Mechanics**: Implemented fast-paced top-down movement where players explore the environment to collect little leaves.
2. **Combat & Defense Systems**: Programmed dynamic combat interactions featuring a swing mechanic to strike enemies and parry incoming projectiles, alongside a responsive dash system to evade enemy attacks.
3. **Jam-Scoped Architecture**: Rapidly prototyped core mechanics under tight game jam constraints while maintaining tight control over player feedback and movement fluidity.

## Final Result

InFaeStation successfully blends arcade-style action mechanics with the jam's organic theme, resulting in an engaging, fast-paced indie prototype.
