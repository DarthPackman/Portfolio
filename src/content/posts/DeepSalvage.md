**Deep Salvage** was built for **Global Game Jam 2025** in Unity. Players dive into open water to collect scattered ship parts, weaving around fish while keeping an eye on a depleting air supply — refilling at intervals to buy enough time to push further down and reach the more valuable wreckage below.

## Core Gameplay Loop

The risk/reward tension comes from balancing depth against air: going deeper means better salvage, but it also means further from a refill and more obstacles to dodge along the way. Managing the air tank turns every dive into a small resource-management decision — push for one more piece of wreckage, or head back up before running out.

## Core Technical & Design Elements

1. **Underwater Movement & Hazard Avoidance**: Built swimming movement and fish-dodging mechanics suited to an open underwater space rather than a traditional platformer or on-rails setup.
2. **Air Management System**: Implemented a depleting air resource that gates how long a player can stay at depth, with refill points that shape the pacing of each dive.
3. **Jam-Scoped Solo Development**: Designed, built, and polished entirely solo within Global Game Jam's tight time constraints.

## Final Result

Deep Salvage delivers a compact, tension-driven diving loop where depth, hazards, and a ticking resource clock combine into a simple but effective risk/reward gameplay hook.
