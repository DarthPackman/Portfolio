**Torn Threads** is a high-mobility first-person movement shooter developed as a university game design project, alongside teammates Laura, Angelo, Vasu, and Alex. The game serves as a homage to *Ender s Game*, blending fast-paced, fluid movement mechanics with a dramatic narrative twist.

## Narrative Concept

The player initially believes they are hooked into a whimsical virtual reality game featuring cartoonish plush enemies, cardboard environments, and a lighthearted atmosphere. However, a hardware malfunction reveals the dark truth: the player has actually been piloting military mech technology to suppress a human rebel faction in the real world. Discarded by their faction after a failure, the player switches sides to join the resistance, fighting their way back through the exact same environments—now stripped of their cheerful disguises to reveal a rugged, war-torn landscape.

## Gameplay & Features

1. **Advanced Movement:** Inspired by titles like *Titanfall* and *Apex Legends*, featuring high-speed traversal with sprinting, double jumping, wall running, air dashing, and a grappling hook.
2. **Combat Arsenal:** Varied gunplay, close-range melee attacks, and throwable tactical items for crowd control.
3. **Dual-Layer Level Design:** Designed across a multi-stage descent and subsequent climb back up, shifting from a toy-like aesthetic to a gritty sci-fi war zone.
